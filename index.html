Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Your creations stay on your device. Photos travel with files only when you choose to export or share. No app account or active ad placements. Advertising and consent SDKs are included but disabled in the default version.

1. About this policy

Draw Music Studio (the “App”), Android package com.khoaidev.drawmusic, is provided by KhoaiStore (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the current App and this policy website. Contact: hvdevelopapplication@gmail.com.

2. Information on your device

The App processes your drawings, musical notes, instruments, tempo, project titles, drafts, saved projects, thumbnails, export history and preferences on your device to let you create, play and manage music. Preferences include language, appearance, sound and vibration choices. The current App has no sign-in, developer-operated cloud synchronization, separate analytics SDK or payment SDK. Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) are included, with initialization disabled by default. There are no ad placements in the current interface. It does not upload your projects to us or collect microphone recordings.

3. Photos and optional camera capture

You may choose a background photo using Android’s photo picker. If you choose to take a photo, the App opens a separate camera app and receives the image you choose to capture. Draw Music Studio does not directly access a live camera feed and does not request camera or broad photo-library permission. Your chosen camera app and photo provider operate under their own settings and policies.

Selected images are resized or converted on-device and can be embedded in saved projects and exported project JSON. A photo background may also appear in a rendered drawing or video. Temporary capture files may be stored in the App’s cache while processing. Inspect personal photos before exporting or sharing a project.

4. Exporting and sharing

The App can render audio and video locally, including WAV, MIDI and MP4, and import or export project files. Generated files and export records may be retained in app-private storage. When you choose to save or share a file, Android’s file picker or sharing tools give access to the destination you select. A destination may be a cloud provider or another app, which handles the file under its own policy. No sharing destination is selected automatically. We do not sell your personal information or use your drawings or music for targeted advertising.

5. Permissions, offline operation and backups

The App uses Internet permission to display the current Privacy Policy and Terms of Use from our GitHub Pages website. Opening these pages sends a normal web request, including your IP address and browser information, to GitHub. No drawing, music project or selected photo is included in that request. If the website cannot load, the App displays its bundled copy. The current App does not request microphone, camera, contacts, location or broad storage permissions. The bundled advertising SDK declares permissions for network connection state, the Android advertising ID and basic phone-state information. These declarations do not enable advertising; the advertising and consent SDKs are disabled by default as described below. Generating music and rendering an MP4 do not record your microphone or screen. Import, save and share actions use Android system tools; those tools and the services you select can have their own network activity.

The App sets Android allowBackup=false and does not offer its own cloud backup. Device transfer behavior can vary by Android version and device manufacturer. Files you save outside the App can still be backed up or synchronized by your chosen provider.

Advertising and privacy choices

The current default version does not initialize the Google advertising or consent SDKs and does not request advertisements. In a version with these services enabled, UMP contacts Google to determine which privacy messages and choices apply. The App checks whether requests are permitted before initializing the advertising SDK. When Google requires a privacy options entry point, it appears in Settings as “Ad privacy choices”.

Google describes the advertising SDK data types, including network addresses, device identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information, in its SDK data disclosure. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Draw Music does not pass your drawings, music projects or selected photos to these SDKs. This policy and the app store disclosures must be updated to describe any future active ad placements before they are released.

6. Retention and deletion

Local projects, drafts, preferences, thumbnails and export records remain until removed using available App controls, cleared in Android Settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled. You can delete saved projects in the Library. Deleting a project does not necessarily remove generated exports or previously shared copies. Temporary cache may be cleaned by the App or Android; there is no single fixed deletion period for all local files.

To reset all app-private data, use Android Settings → Apps → Draw Music Studio → Storage → Clear storage/data. This deletes your local work and settings. Exported files in shared storage, cloud destinations and copies held by recipients can remain after uninstalling. Delete those through your file manager or the relevant service. There is no online App account to delete, and we cannot remotely access or erase files stored only on your device.

7. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments. Our email provider processes those messages. We use them to respond, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests, and keep them as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. We may disclose correspondence where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and security. Please do not send private project files unless needed for your request.

8. Security and your rights

App-private data uses Android’s storage protections. Protect your device and review files before sharing them; no storage method guarantees absolute security. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw applicable consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us about information you have sent to us. We may need to verify your request. Local-only data is controlled on your device.

Where a legal basis is required, handling your support request relies on our legitimate interest in assisting users, subject to your rights, or on applicable legal obligations. We do not treat acceptance of the Terms as permission for unrelated optional data processing.

9. Children

The App does not require children to submit a name, contact information or an online profile. Parents and guardians should supervise photo selection, external camera use and sharing. If you believe a child has sent us personal information without the permission required by law, contact us so we can review and delete it where required. This statement does not replace protections required by applicable law.

10. This website and international processing

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, cookies, ads or submission forms to these pages. GitHub may process request details such as IP address to operate and secure hosting; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting, email and services you choose may process information outside your country under their own policies and applicable safeguards. External links lead to services with their own practices.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when practices change, and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. This policy describes the current default version with advertising services disabled; enabling those services or adding paid features requires updated disclosures before release.
